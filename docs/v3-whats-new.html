Clock that runs itself

Starts automatically and covers the whole workpack. It asks before you leave, and at sign-in it prompts you if a clock was left running.

🔑

PIN self-service

Reset your PIN with an emailed code. Supervisors can set, clear or unlock PINs.

🧠

Remembers you

Keeps your theme, your filters and your last work order. One tap resumes where you left off.

Agentforce in the header

Open the chat from any screen and pin it to the side. Answers come with charts and cards.

Supervisor tools

Config and Time Tracking are shown to supervisors only.
